How it works

Set it up once. It runs every day.

01

Tell us how your business works

A two-minute onboarding — your business name, what you do, what you don't, and team size. That is the only form you ever fill in manually.

02

Connect your tools

Start with Slack and Gmail — the two places where most decisions actually happen. We also import the last six months of your Slack history, so memory starts full, not empty. For anything else — HubSpot, Notion, Pipedrive, any spreadsheet — upload a CSV or Excel export and it's in. Direct native connections to project management tools are coming in the next phase.

03

It reads, learns, and builds your memory daily

Every day, EarlyEcho reads new messages, emails, and uploaded documents. Facts your team clearly stated are stored automatically. Anything uncertain or conflicting is held for your team to review. You stay in control of what enters memory.

04

Ask. Brief. Act.

Query your memory in plain language and get cited answers — every response traces back to the exact source it came from. Every morning, a briefing tells your team what matters before the day starts. Give the agent a task — a proposal, a client brief — and it works only from what has been verified.

How accuracy works

Every fact is checked before it enters memory

A dedicated review layer reads every extraction before anything is stored. It asks: is this actually stated in the source? Does it conflict with what the team already knows? Facts above a confidence threshold are stored automatically — no interruption. Everything else comes to your team for a one-click decision.
