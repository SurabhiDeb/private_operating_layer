# EarlyEcho — Client Demo Script
**Target length: 3–4 minutes**

---

## OPENING — The Problem (30 sec)
*[No screen recording yet. Just you, talking to camera or voiceover.]*

"Every team I've spoken to has the same problem.

You make decisions in Slack. You close deals over email. You build processes in people's heads. And six months later, when someone asks 'what did we decide about this client?' or 'what's our policy on that?' — nobody knows. Someone has to dig through threads, ask around, or just guess.

That's not a people problem. It's a memory problem.

EarlyEcho fixes it."

---

## PART 1 — The Brain (60 sec)
*[Open the dashboard. Clean, no setup screens.]*

"This is your company's brain.

Everything your team has ever decided, believed, or agreed on — searchable in one place.

Let me show you what I mean."

*[Click into the Ask a Question card. Type: "What is our policy on client contracts?"]*

"I'm going to ask it something a new team member would normally have to hunt for."

*[Answer appears with a cited source — Slack channel, date, link]*

"There's the answer. And notice — it tells you exactly where it came from. A Slack message from three weeks ago. You can click through to the original.

This isn't a chatbot guessing. It's your own company's knowledge, returned to you."

---

## PART 2 — Human Approval (45 sec)
*[Navigate to the Approval Queue card]*

"Now here's what makes this different from every other tool.

Nothing goes into memory automatically. Every piece of information the AI extracts from your Slack or email gets reviewed by your team before it's stored.

This is your approval queue."

*[Show a pending item — approve one]*

"You see what it extracted, why it flagged it, and how confident it is. You approve it, reject it, or edit it. Only then does it become part of your company's memory.

You stay in control. The AI doesn't decide what's true — your team does."

---

## PART 3 — Morning Briefing (30 sec)
*[Navigate to the Morning Briefing card. Click Load Briefing.]*

"Every morning, your team gets a briefing — written from your actual company memory, not from a template.

Open client decisions. Pipeline updates. Things your team agreed on last week that are worth remembering today.

And you can tell it exactly what to focus on — sales, operations, client delivery — whatever matters most to your team right now."

---

## PART 4 — The Moat (30 sec)
*[Stay on dashboard or cut back to camera]*

"One last thing — and this matters.

This doesn't run on shared infrastructure. It doesn't train on your data. It doesn't sit in a multi-tenant cloud where your information sits next to a hundred other companies.

EarlyEcho runs on a private server built only for you. Your data never leaves it.

That's not a feature. That's the foundation."

---

## CLOSE (15 sec)
*[Back to camera]*

"If your team's knowledge is scattered — we can fix that.

[Your name], EarlyEcho."

---

## Notes for recording
- Use real data in the demo, not placeholder text. The cited answers are your strongest visual.
- Pause after the answer appears in Part 1 — let it land before you speak.
- Keep the approval queue section slow and deliberate — this is your biggest differentiator, don't rush it.
- Don't show registration, Slack setup, or CSV upload screens — cut straight to the working product.
